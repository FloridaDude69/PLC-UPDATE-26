❓ QUESTIONS YOU MIGHT HAVE

Q: Where is the shortcut?
A: On your desktop! Look for "PLC Update Manager" icon

Q: What if I can't find the shortcut?
A: Check: C:\Users\634647\OneDrive - TMNA\Desktop\
   or right-click desktop and look in folder view

Q: How long does it take to start?
A: Usually 10-30 seconds for the app to build and start

Q: What's that PowerShell terminal that opens?
A: That's the web server running. Keep it open while you use the app.

Q: How do I stop the application?
A: In the PowerShell window, press Ctrl+C (hold Control, press C)

Q: Can I run it again later?
A: Yes! Just click the shortcut again anytime

Q: Does it need internet?
A: No! It runs locally on your computer

Q: What URL do I use?
A: http://localhost:5034/file-updates

Q: Can other people access it?
A: Only on your local computer by default
