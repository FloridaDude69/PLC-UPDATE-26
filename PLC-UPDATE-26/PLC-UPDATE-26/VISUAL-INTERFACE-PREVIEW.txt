ANIMATION EXAMPLES

Header Gradient Shift (8s loop):
  0%   →  background-position: 0% 50%
  50%  →  background-position: 100% 50%
  100% →  background-position: 0% 50%

  Result: Smooth color wave flowing left to right continuously

Icon Float Animation (3s loop):
  0%, 100%  →  transform: translateY(0px)
  50%       →  transform: translateY(-15px)

  Result: Icons gently float up and down

Card Shimmer (3s loop):
  Movement pattern creates moving light effect across card
  Result: Elegant shine effect on stat cards

Hover Lift Animation:
  Duration: 0.4s
  Easing: cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)
  Transform: translateY(-15px) scale(1.02)
  Result: Smooth, natural card lift with glow
